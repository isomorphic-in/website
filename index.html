Institutional Governance

FERPA-Ready Architecture. 100% Isolated Data.

We treat institutional data, student privacy, and campus communications with the highest standard of enterprise compliance and security.

🔒 FERPA & Student Privacy Compliance: Student inquiries and session logs are safeguarded in private institutional containers with strict role-based access controls.
🚫 No Public LLM Training: Your academic catalogs, internal policies, and student conversations are strictly confidential and never used to train public foundation models.
🎯 Deterministic Grounding: The AI assistant is mathematically constrained to only cite facts from your approved institutional documents, eliminating hallucinations.
🛡️ Automated PII Redaction: Student identification numbers, contact information, and sensitive records are masked prior to analytical indexing.
